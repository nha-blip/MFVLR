## 3. Execution & Environment Bottleneck Analysis

In addition to checkpoint acquisition, FSLSD requires:
1. **Linux NCCL Distributed Backend:** `test.py` strictly initializes `torch.distributed.init_process_group(backend="nccl", init_method="env://")`, which is unsupported on Windows.
2. **CUDA JIT C++ Compilation:** Uses rosinality's StyleGAN2 operations (`op/fused_act.py` and `op/upfirdn2d.py`), requiring Linux GCC + CUDA Toolkit.
